# Fourteen Stations: Apple Music + DJs

Fourteen fan-made radio stations. The songs stream from Apple Music through the listener's own account; the DJ breaks between them play from this site. Refreshed monthly.

- Non-commercial, not monetized, not affiliated with Apple.
- The hosts are fictional characters. Their voices are synthetic, cloned from people who consented to share their speech: Meta Expresso and EARS datasets, licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Song previews and playback come from Apple Music; each song links to its Apple Music page.
