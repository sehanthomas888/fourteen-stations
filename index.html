Radio Rosetta

Apple Music edition. Pick a station: the songs stream from Apple Music, and the DJs talk between them, naming what you just heard and what's coming next. Prefer Spotify or YouTube? All stations on every app.

How it works

The DJ breaks play from this page. The songs come from Apple Music through your own account, so you need an Apple Music subscription for full songs. Nothing about you is stored here.

Fresh every month

Every station gets new songs and new DJ breaks at the start of each month. The link stays the same.

About the voices

The hosts are fictional. Their voices are synthetic, cloned from people who consented to share their speech (Meta Expresso and EARS datasets, CC BY-NC 4.0; Masala 98.3's hosts: AI4Bharat IndicVoices-R, CC BY 4.0; Riddim, Fuego, Pirate 140 and Warehouse 124: the Edinburgh International Accents of English Corpus, CC BY-SA 4.0).
