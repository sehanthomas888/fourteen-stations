Radio Rosetta

Fourteen stations, one dial. Pick a frequency and press play. Every station is 24 hand-picked songs with a DJ break every four tracks, and the DJs name what you just heard and what's coming next. It's free on YouTube (no account needed) and on Spotify, DJs included. On Apple Music? Use the Apple Music + DJs player.

Where the DJs live

The full show, with DJ breaks, plays on Spotify, YouTube and YouTube Music. On Spotify every DJ break is a short episode of the Radio Rosetta podcast, placed between the songs in the station's playlist: play it in order with shuffle off. On Apple Music, use the Apple Music + DJs player: every DJ break is there now, songs play as 30-second previews for the moment, and full songs through your own Apple Music are coming. The plain Apple Music playlists carry the songs without the DJs, because Apple never shares audio that isn't in its own catalogue.

Fresh every month

Each station gets new songs and new DJ breaks at the start of every month. The links on this page stay the same, so bookmark it and come back.

About the voices

The hosts are fictional characters. Their voices are synthetic, cloned from people who consented to share their speech (Meta Expresso and EARS datasets, CC BY-NC 4.0). Non-commercial, not monetized.
